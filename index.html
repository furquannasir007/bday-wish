A letter for you

To the girl who makes my world softer

Dear Aeram Shamim,

Today is not just another date on the calendar. It is the day Allah chose to bring you into this world — and somehow, years later, He brought you into mine too. I don't think I will ever have enough words to explain how special that feels to me.

You have this beautiful way of making ordinary moments feel important. Your smile, your voice, the way you talk, the little things you do without even realizing them — they all have a place in my heart. When I talk to you, even a normal day feels a little less ordinary.

I know that today I cannot be beside you the way I wish I could. And honestly, that distance hurts a little more on a day like this. Kaash aaj main aapke paas hota, aapke saamne baithta, aapko birthday wish karta, aur aapke chehre ki woh smile apni aankhon se dekhta.

But until the day distance becomes a story we tell about the past, I want you to know this: no matter how many kilometres are between us, you are never far from my heart.

May Allah fill your life with happiness, protect you from every bad thing and every bad person, give you success in everything you work for, and keep your heart peaceful. Aameen summa aameen.

And if I could make one birthday wish for myself, it would simply be this — that I get to celebrate many, many more of your birthdays with you.

Happy Birthday, my love. ❤️
